# SoundStage

**SoundStage** is a web-based application designed to manage music concerts effectively by digitalizing the processes of event creation, ticket booking, online payments, and attendee management. The system is created to target two categories of users: **Music Concert Organizers** and **Attendees**.

## Description

The SoundStage platform allows music concert organizers to create and manage music concert events with ease. Organizers can establish ticket categories, set ticket prices, and track ticket sales. On the other hand, participants (attendees) can search for upcoming music concerts in other cities, purchase tickets online, and pay using digital wallets like **eSewa** and **Khalti**. After purchasing a ticket, attendees will receive an e-ticket in the form of a QR code.

The concert organizers will be able to scan the QR code at the venue to verify the validity of the ticket and obtain the attendee's information, ensuring a smooth, secure, and fast entry process at the concert venue.

## Features

### For Music Concert Organizers:
- **Event Creation and Management**: Easily create and manage concert events.
- **Ticket Categories and Pricing**: Set different ticket categories and prices.
- **Sales Tracking**: Keep track of ticket sales for better event management.

### For Attendees:
- **Concert Search**: Search for music concerts happening in various cities.
- **Online Ticket Booking**: Purchase tickets online and make payments using digital wallets like **eSewa** and **Khalti**.
- **e-Ticket with QR Code**: Get an e-ticket in the form of a QR code after purchase.
- **QR Code Scanning**: Concert organizers can scan the QR code at the venue to verify ticket validity and attendee details.
